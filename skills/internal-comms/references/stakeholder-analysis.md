# Stakeholder analysis — the power/interest grid

Before you write a single announcement, you need to know **who** you are
talking to and **how** each group should be engaged. Mendelow's power/interest
grid is the workhorse tool. This is the logic behind
`scripts/stakeholder_matrix.py`.

## The two axes
- **Power:** the stakeholder's ability to affect the change — to fund it, veto
  it, slow it, or accelerate it.
- **Interest:** how much the stakeholder cares about and is affected by the
  change.

Rating each 1-5 and cutting at a high/low threshold yields four quadrants.

## The four quadrants and their strategy
| Quadrant | Power | Interest | Strategy | Cadence & channel |
|----------|-------|----------|----------|-------------------|
| Manage Closely | High | High | Partner; co-create | Weekly 1:1 / steering group |
| Keep Satisfied | High | Low | Keep on side, don't overload | Biweekly executive briefing |
| Keep Informed | Low | High | Involve, use as champions | Newsletter / demos |
| Monitor | Low | Low | Minimal effort, watch for change | Broadcast / self-serve FAQ |

## Why mis-targeting is the common failure
Two symmetric mistakes waste effort and credibility:
- **Over-communicating** to Monitor or Keep-Satisfied groups — flooding a
  low-interest executive with detail, or a low/low group with updates they
  never read.
- **Under-communicating** to Manage-Closely groups — broadcasting to a
  high-power, high-interest sponsor who needed a conversation. These are the
  people who sink a change when they feel blindsided.

## Dynamics — the grid is not static
Power and interest move. A regulator's interest spikes when an incident occurs;
a middle manager's power rises when they are named to the steering group. Re-run
the analysis at each phase, and watch Keep-Satisfied and Monitor groups for
rising interest that should promote them to closer management.

## From grid to communication plan
The quadrant sets cadence and channel; it does not write the message. Pair this
analysis with ADKAR (what does this audience need — awareness? ability?) and
with the change-communication planning reference to turn the grid into a dated
plan with owners.
